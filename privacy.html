Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

The short version

Paster keeps your clipboard history on your Mac. We don't have servers that receive it, we don't sell data, and there are no ads or trackers.

Your clipboard data

Everything Paster saves — text, links, images, files you copied, pinboards and settings — is stored only in Paster's container on your Mac. Text inside images is recognised on your Mac using Apple's Vision framework. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

By default Paster skips content that apps mark as confidential (such as passwords) and content from apps you choose to ignore. You can pause saving at any time and erase your history in Settings. Deleting the app removes its data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To manage subscriptions, Paster uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history and a random, anonymous app user ID generated on your Mac. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising.

What we don't collect

No account, no name, no email, no contacts, no location, no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and none of your clipboard content.

Children

Paster is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions? Contact us.
