Getting started

Open your clipboard

Press ⇧⌘V in any app. Use the arrow keys to pick an item and Return to paste it. ⇧Return pastes plain text, ⌘1–9 pastes one of the first nine items.

Search

Just start typing. Paster searches everything you copied, including text inside screenshots and images.

Pinboards

Right-click a card and choose Pin to, or drag it onto a pinboard tab. Pinned items never expire.

Paste Queue

Press ⇧⌘C, copy a few things, then press ⌘V where you need them — each paste takes the next item.

Permissions

Accessibility lets Paster press ⌘V for you so an item lands right where your cursor is. Without it, Paster copies the item and you paste it yourself.

Input Monitoring is only used while the Paste Queue is open, to notice each ⌘V and move to the next item.

Both are in System Settings → Privacy & Security. If paste stops working after a macOS update, remove Paster from the list and add it again.

Subscription

Paster Pro is available weekly (with a 7-day free trial) or yearly. To cancel or change your plan, open the App Store → your account → Manage Subscriptions, or go there directly. Bought on another Mac? Use Restore Purchases in Paster → Settings → Subscription.
